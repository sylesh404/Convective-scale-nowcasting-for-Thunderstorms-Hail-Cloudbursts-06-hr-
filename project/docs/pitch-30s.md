**Product:** NOWCAST AI — Convective Scale Weather Intelligence

### The Pitch
> "Severe convective thunderstorms, hail, and cloudbursts develop in under an hour, causing catastrophic loss of life and infrastructure across India before traditional weather models can even finish running.
> 
> We built **NOWCAST AI** — an operational multi-source nowcasting platform that fuses Doppler radar, INSAT-3D satellite imagery, lightning detection, and NWP into an ultra-fast ConvLSTM neural network.
> 
> In just **14.8 milliseconds**, it predicts storm tracks and hazard intensities across the next 0 to 6 hours and pinpoints the exact schools, hospitals, and highways at risk — turning raw meteorological telemetry into actionable, life-saving intelligence."
